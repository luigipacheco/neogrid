## Fabnodes: Digital Fabrication in Blender

**Fabnodes** is a Blender add-on that exports **G-code** directly from **Geometry Nodes**. It lets you create parametric toolpaths for **CNC machining, 3D printing, laser cutting, and pen plotting** inside Blender — no separate CAM software needed. Because the toolpaths are procedural, you can adjust the design and the fabrication strategy together, in the same node tree.

### Key Features

- **G-code export** for CNC machines, 3D printers, laser cutters, and plotters.
- **Geometry Nodes integration** — toolpaths are just geometry, built procedurally.
- **Flexible workflows** — adjust toolpaths to the machine and material at hand.
- **Customizable** — modify and optimize everything without leaving Blender.
