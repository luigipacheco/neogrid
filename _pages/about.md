# About Me

<p class="tagline">
  <strong>Computational design &middot; Digital fabrication &middot; Robotics</strong><br>
  Assistant Professor of Architecture at Florida Atlantic University &middot; Director, Interactive Machines Lab
</p>

<div class="about-layout" markdown="1">

![Luis Pacheco](/assets/images/portrait.jpg){: .about-portrait}

<div markdown="1">
I am an Assistant Professor of Architecture at Florida Atlantic University and Director of the Interactive Machines Lab, where my research explores the intersection of computational design, robotics, and digital fabrication. My work focuses on developing intuitive robot control interfaces for creative professionals and advancing innovative workflows for digital manufacturing in construction. I am also a Doctor of Design candidate (2025) at FIU, with research centered on procedural robot control and integration interfaces in architectural robotics.

I am the creator of Animaquina, a Blender add-on for controlling robotic arms, and founder of Neobrutal Studio, a consulting practice focused on additive manufacturing and robotics. I previously co-founded and served as CEO of MakerMex, Mexico's first 3D printer manufacturing company, where I led the development of 3D printers, laser cutters, and custom robotic systems. I also co-founded MakerLab León and MakerScad, initiatives promoting open-source technologies and digital fabrication.

I hold a master's degree in Robotics and Advanced Construction from IAAC in Barcelona and a bachelor's degree in architecture from Universidad de La Salle Bajío in Mexico. In 2016, I was recognized as one of Mexico's MIT Innovators Under 35.
